## What is required

Pavements should be intact and even. As per [IRC:82-2015](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.082.2015.pdf) (maintenance of bituminous surfaces) and IRC:SP-73:

- **No potholes** that expose the base or cause significant ride discomfort or safety risk.
- **Cracks** (longitudinal, transverse, or block) should be sealed or treated before they widen and allow water ingress, which leads to potholes.
- **Edge breaks** and **ravelling** (loose aggregate) should be repaired so the surface remains stable.

Even small potholes can grow quickly in monsoon. Timely repair with proper preparation, compaction, and material (bituminous or cold mix as applicable) is expected.

## What is often missing or wrong

- **Potholes** of various sizes, some deep enough to damage vehicles or cause two-wheeler accidents.
- **Unrepaired cracks** that have widened or joined, indicating that the surface is failing.
- **Patches** that have failed or sunk, or that were done without proper compaction (leading to quick recurrence).
- **No visible repair** on roads that have been in bad condition for a long time.

If you see potholes, large cracks, or failed patches that make the road unsafe or uncomfortable, that is reportable. Repairs should be **structural** (minimum depth to sound pavement, typically **at least 40 mm** per IRC:82-2015) — see [IRC pothole repair standards](/knowledge/irc/pothole-repair) for procedure and [IRC:82-2015](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.082.2015.pdf) for the full code.

## Who to report to

**GHMC** is responsible for most city roads in Hyderabad. Major roads under the Comprehensive Road Maintenance Programme (CRMP) are maintained by contracted agencies on behalf of GHMC. Report cracks and potholes via the [GHMC complaint portal](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint) or helpline **21111111**. You can also use FixMyRoad to [report an issue](/report) with location and photo.
