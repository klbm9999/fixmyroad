## What is required

- **Street lights** should be provided on major roads, junctions, and in areas where visibility at night is important for safety (e.g. near schools, markets, bus stops).
- Lights should be **functional** (not burnt out or damaged) and **adequately spaced** so that the road is lit.
- **Poles and fittings** should be intact (not bent, broken, or dangling) and wiring should be safe.

GHMC is responsible for street lighting on municipal roads in Hyderabad; maintenance may be done by the corporation or through contracts.

## What is often missing or wrong

- **Non-working lights** — bulbs or LEDs not lit for long periods.
- **Missing lights** on stretches that are dark and used by vehicles or pedestrians at night.
- **Damaged poles or fittings** — bent, broken, or hazardous.
- **Lights not switched on** or on wrong timings (e.g. off when it is already dark).
- **Inconsistent spacing** — long dark gaps between working lights.

If you notice a stretch with no lights or many non-working lights that affects safety, that is reportable.

## Who to report to

**GHMC** is responsible for street lighting on city roads in Hyderabad. Report non-working or missing lights via the [GHMC complaint portal](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint) or helpline **21111111**. You can also use FixMyRoad to [report an issue](/report) with location and, if possible, a photo showing the dark or non-working stretch.
