## What is required

As per [IRC:SP-73](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.sp.073.2018.pdf) and good practice (cross-fall **2–2.5%** for bituminous surfaces):

- **Surface drainage**: The road should have **cross-fall** (slope across the width) so that water runs off to the sides and does not pond on the carriageway.
- **Side drains** (open or covered) along the road to carry runoff away. They should be **clear** of silt, garbage, and debris.
- **Culverts** and cross-drains where water crosses under the road; they must be **free-flowing** and not blocked.
- **No prolonged waterlogging** on the carriageway or shoulder after rain, which accelerates pavement damage and causes potholes.

Proper drainage extends pavement life and reduces monsoon damage.

## What is often missing or wrong

- **Blocked side drains** — filled with silt, plastic, or waste so that water overflows onto the road.
- **No drain** or **collapsed drain** along stretches where runoff has nowhere to go.
- **Waterlogging** on the road or shoulder after normal rain, indicating poor cross-fall or blocked outlets.
- **Culverts blocked** or too small, causing overflow and flooding.
- **Debris or encroachments** in the drain line.

If you see blocked or missing drains, or regular waterlogging that damages the road or creates hazard, that is reportable.

## Who to report to

**GHMC** is responsible for drainage along city roads in Hyderabad. Stormwater drains and culverts are part of road maintenance (including under CRMP contracts). Report blocked or missing drainage and waterlogging via the [GHMC complaint portal](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint) or helpline **21111111**. You can also [report an issue](/report) on FixMyRoad with location and photo.
