## What is required

As per [IRC](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.sp.073.2018.pdf) and traffic guidelines:

### Pedestrian crossings

- **Zebra crossings**: Black-and-white striped markings at designated crossing points (e.g. near schools, markets, bus stops, hospitals). Should be clearly visible and maintained.
- **Signalised crossings**: Where traffic signals control right-of-way, pedestrian phases and clear markings (including stop lines) are expected.
- **Stop lines**: Solid white line for vehicles to stop before the crossing so pedestrians can use the facility safely.

### Non-pedestrian crossings

- **Railway level crossings**: Where roads meet railway lines, proper signage, barriers or gates, and marking as per railway and road authorities.
- **Cattle / animal crossings**: On rural or semi-urban stretches, warning signs and sometimes dedicated crossing points where applicable.
- **Wildlife crossings**: In relevant zones, structures or signing as per forest/wildlife and road authorities.

Crossings should be visible, maintained, and consistent with IRC and MoRTH guidelines for the road category.

## What is often missing or wrong

- **Faded or missing zebra markings** at busy pedestrian locations.
- **No stop line** or stop line too far from the crossing.
- **Missing or non-functional traffic signals** at signalised crossings.
- **No advance warning signs** for pedestrian or non-pedestrian crossings (e.g. "Pedestrian crossing ahead", "Railway crossing").
- **Unsafe or blocked** crossing area (encroachments, waterlogging, damaged kerb).

If you see a crossing that is missing, faded, or unsafe where one is clearly needed, that is reportable.

## Who to report to

**GHMC** (or the road-owning agency) is responsible for road markings and traffic facilities on municipal roads in Hyderabad, including pedestrian crossings and associated signage. Report missing or unsafe crossings via the [GHMC complaint portal](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint) or helpline **21111111**. For railway crossings, the concerned railway division may also need to be contacted for gates/signals.
