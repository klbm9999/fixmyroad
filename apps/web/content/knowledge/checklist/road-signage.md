## What is required

As per IRC and traffic sign standards (see [IRC:SP-73](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.sp.073.2018.pdf) and MoRTH/state sign manuals):

- **Mandatory signs** (e.g. speed limit, no entry, one-way) must be posted where the rule applies, with correct size and placement so they are visible to drivers.
- **Caution/warning signs** (e.g. curve, school ahead, pedestrian crossing) should be placed in advance of the hazard.
- **Informatory signs** (e.g. direction, facility, place name) help navigation and should be present at junctions and along important routes.
- Signs must be **readable** (not faded, bent, or obscured by vegetation or other obstructions) and **correctly oriented**.

Missing or wrong signs can lead to wrong driver behaviour and accidents.

## What is often missing or wrong

- **Missing mandatory signs** (e.g. no speed limit sign where limits apply, no “school ahead” near schools).
- **Damaged or faded signs** that cannot be read.
- **Signs obscured** by trees, hoardings, or parked vehicles.
- **Wrong or outdated** informatory signs (e.g. wrong place name or direction).
- **No sign** after a new road or junction is built.

If you notice a sign that is missing, illegible, damaged, or misleading, it is worth reporting.

## Who to report to

- **Road signage (placement, condition, missing)** on GHMC-maintained roads: **GHMC** — [How to lodge a complaint](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint), helpline **21111111**.
- **Traffic rules and enforcement** (e.g. whether a sign is legally in force): **RTO / Transport Department** — see [RTO contacts](/knowledge/rto/rto-contacts). For physical installation and maintenance of signs on city roads, GHMC remains the primary contact.
