## What is required

- **Traffic signals** (red / amber / green) at busy intersections to control right-of-way and reduce conflicts.
- **Placement and visibility**: Signals should be clearly visible to approaching traffic (correct height, position, and not obscured by vegetation or hoardings).
- **Functioning**: All aspects (red, amber, green) should work; timings should be appropriate for the traffic and pedestrian flow.
- **Pedestrian phases**: Where applicable, pedestrian signals or phases should be provided and working.
- **Consistency**: Signalling should match lane layout and signage (e.g. no conflicting signs).

IRC and traffic engineering guidelines specify where signals are warranted and how they should be installed and maintained.

## What is often missing or wrong

- **Non-working signals** — one or more aspects not lit, or stuck on one phase.
- **Missing signals** at busy junctions where conflict and delay are high.
- **Obscured signals** — trees, hoardings, or poles blocking the view.
- **Out of sync or wrong timings** — causing confusion or unsafe behaviour.
- **No pedestrian phase** where pedestrian flow is high and crossing is unsafe without it.

If you notice traffic signals that are missing, not working, or hard to see at an intersection, that is reportable.

## Who to report to

**GHMC** (or the road authority for that stretch) is responsible for traffic signals on city roads in Hyderabad. Report non-working or missing signals via the [GHMC complaint portal](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint) or helpline **21111111**. You can also [report an issue](/report) on FixMyRoad with location and, if possible, a photo.
