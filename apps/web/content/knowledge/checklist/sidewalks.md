## What is required

As per IRC and urban road guidelines:

- **Footpaths / sidewalks** should be provided along roads in built-up areas so that pedestrians can walk safely off the carriageway.
- **Width**: Sufficient for pedestrian flow (IRC and local guidelines specify minimum widths for different road categories; e.g. **1.5 m** minimum in many urban contexts).
- **Continuity**: Footpaths should be continuous where pedestrian movement is expected, with proper kerb and drop kerbs at crossings.
- **Surface**: Even, non-slippery, and free of obstructions. Damaged or broken slabs and potholes on footpaths are hazards.
- **Clear of encroachments**: Stalls, parked vehicles, and debris should not block the walkway.

Proper sidewalks reduce pedestrian–vehicle conflict and improve safety.

## What is often missing or wrong

- **No footpath** on stretches where pedestrians are present (e.g. near markets, schools, bus stops).
- **Broken or uneven surface** — cracked slabs, potholes, or missing sections.
- **Encroachments** — vendors, parked vehicles, or construction material blocking the path.
- **No kerb or drop kerb** at crossing points, making access difficult for wheelchair users or prams.
- **Narrow or discontinuous** footpath that forces pedestrians onto the road.

If you see missing, damaged, or encroached sidewalks that put pedestrians at risk, that is reportable.

## Who to report to

**GHMC** is responsible for footpaths and pedestrian facilities on municipal roads in Hyderabad. Report missing, broken, or encroached sidewalks via the [GHMC complaint portal](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint) or helpline **21111111**. You can also [report an issue](/report) on FixMyRoad with location and photo.
