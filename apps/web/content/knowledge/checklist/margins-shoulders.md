## What is required

As per [IRC:SP-73 (2018)](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.sp.073.2018.pdf) (e.g. **2.5 m** paved shoulder for two-lane highways with paved shoulder):

- **Shoulders** (the strip between the carriageway edge and the boundary) should have a defined width and surface so that vehicles can pull off safely and drainage is not blocked.
- **Verges** (area beyond the shoulder) should be clear of obstructions and encroachments so that sight lines and drainage are maintained.
- Shoulders should be **stable** (no large potholes or erosion) and **clearly distinguishable** from the carriageway where relevant.

On urban roads, “margins” may include footpaths, planting strips, or paved strips beside the carriageway. These should be maintained and free of dangerous encroachments.

## What is often missing or wrong

- **No proper shoulder** — edge of road drops off or is unpaved and unusable.
- **Shoulder damaged or eroded** — potholes, subsidence, or washouts.
- **Encroachments** on margins (e.g. stalls, debris, construction material) reducing effective width or blocking drainage.
- **Overgrown vegetation** obscuring signs or narrowing the usable width.
- **No clear boundary** between carriageway and margin, so drivers cannot safely pull off.

If you see missing, damaged, or encroached margins or shoulders that affect safety or drainage, that is reportable.

## Who to report to

**GHMC** (or the road-owning agency) is responsible for road margins and shoulders on municipal roads in Hyderabad. Report issues via the [GHMC complaint portal](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint) or helpline **21111111**. For [who maintains which roads](/knowledge/ghmc/who-maintains-what), see the GHMC and CRMP responsibility breakdown.
