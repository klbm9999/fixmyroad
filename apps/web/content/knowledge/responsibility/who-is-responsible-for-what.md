Use this as a quick reference for **who maintains what** and **who to contact** for road-related issues in Hyderabad.

## By road type

| Road type                                      | Maintaining agency                   | Who to contact                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Internal roads** (lanes, bylanes, ~6,900 km) | GHMC (direct)                        | GHMC — helpline 21111111, [complaint portal](https://igs.ghmc.gov.in) |
| **Major city roads under CRMP** (1,700+ km)    | Private agencies under GHMC contract | GHMC — same as above; GHMC routes to contractor                       |
| **Certain major corridors** (e.g. ORR)         | HMDA or its contractors              | HMDA / or lodge with GHMC for direction                               |
| **State/national highways** (if any in limits) | PWD / NHAI / state authority         | Respective authority (not GHMC)                                       |

## By type of issue

| Issue                                       | Primary contact      | Notes                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Potholes, cracks, road surface**          | GHMC                 | Same for internal and CRMP roads; GHMC/contractor fixes |
| **Drainage (blocked drains, waterlogging)** | GHMC                 | Part of road maintenance / CRMP                         |
| **Lane markings, edge lines, zebra**        | GHMC                 | Road-owning agency                                      |
| **Road signage (missing, damaged)**         | GHMC                 | Physical sign installation and maintenance              |
| **Speed limit signs**                       | GHMC (installation)  | RTO enforces rules; GHMC maintains signs on city roads  |
| **Street lighting**                         | GHMC                 | On municipal roads                                      |
| **Encroachments on road/margins**           | GHMC                 | Removal and enforcement                                 |
| **Traffic rules, licences, fines**          | RTO / Transport Dept | [RTO contacts](/knowledge/rto/rto-contacts)             |

## RTO vs GHMC

- **GHMC** (or its contractors): **Build and maintain** the road — surface, drains, lights, markings, signs on city roads. **Lodge complaints** for potholes, drainage, lights, signage, encroachments with GHMC (21111111, [complaint portal](https://igs.ghmc.gov.in)).
- **RTO / Transport**: **Rules and enforcement** — licences, registration, traffic fines, compliance with Motor Vehicles Act. **Does not maintain** the physical road. For road defects, contact GHMC.
