Indian Road Congress (IRC) documents provide guidance on pavement maintenance and pothole repair: **IRC:SP-73** (2018) for two-lane highways with paved shoulder ([view IRC:SP-73](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.sp.073.2018.pdf)) and **IRC:82-2015** (Code of Practice for Maintenance of Bituminous Road Surfaces) for materials, methods, and equipment ([view IRC:82-2015](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.082.2015.pdf)).

## Preparation

- **Define the repair area**: Remove loose material and cut back to sound pavement. The patch should be rectangular or regular in shape with vertical or slightly undercut edges so the new material keys in.
- **Clean the patch**: Remove all loose aggregate, dust, and water. The surface should be dry and clean before placing new material.
- **Tack coat**: Apply bituminous tack coat on the vertical sides (and base if required) so that the new mix bonds to the existing pavement.

## Materials

- **Bituminous mix** (hot or warm mix) is preferred for durable repairs where hot mix is available and the area is large enough to be economical.
- **Cold mix** may be used for small, quick repairs or where hot mix is not practical. It should conform to IRC specifications; storage and application should be as per the manufacturer and code.
- **Aggregate** should be of specified grading and quality; binder content and compaction are critical for longevity.

## Compaction and finish

- **Compaction**: The patch must be compacted to the required density (typically with a roller or plate compactor) so that it does not sink or ravel under traffic.
- **Finish**: The repaired surface should be flush with the surrounding pavement and sealed at the edges to prevent water ingress.

## Minimum dimensions

**IRC:82-2015** (Section 10 — Materials and Methods for Rectification of Distress) specifies that a pothole repair should be **structural**, not just a surface fill. The repair depth must extend to **sound pavement** (typically at least **40 mm** for a bituminous overlay so the patch keys into the base; shallow fills tend to fail quickly). The patch area should be cut back to a regular shape (rectangular or square) with vertical or slightly undercut edges. For exact minimum depth and area values in your road category, see [IRC:82-2015, Section 10](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/irc/irc.gov.in.082.2015.pdf).

Application temperatures, material specifications, and equipment are given in IRC:82-2015 (Section 11) and IRC:SP-73. The guideline last updated date above refers to the IRC document revision.
