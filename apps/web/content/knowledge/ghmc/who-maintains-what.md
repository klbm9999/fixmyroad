In Hyderabad, road maintenance is split between **GHMC** (directly or through contractors) and, for some major corridors, **HMDA** (Hyderabad Metropolitan Development Authority).

## GHMC — majority of city roads

- **GHMC** (Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation) is the road-owning and coordinating authority for most municipal roads in Hyderabad.
- **~6,900 km** of roads are **internal roads** (single-lane lanes and bylanes). These are maintained **directly by GHMC** (departmentally or through local contracts).
- **Over 1,700 km** of **major roads** are under the **Comprehensive Road Maintenance Programme (CRMP)**. These are maintained by **private agencies** under contract to GHMC. GHMC retains ownership and oversight; the contractor is responsible for day-to-day maintenance, pothole repair, re-carpeting as per contract, drainage cleaning, and related works.
- CRMP has been implemented in phases (e.g. Phase I around 812 km; Phase II adding more length). Under CRMP Phase II, additional major roads may be brought under contract.

## HMDA

- **HMDA** is responsible for metropolitan-level planning and some major infrastructure. It may own or manage certain **major corridors** (e.g. Outer Ring Road) and associated signage and development. For those stretches, HMDA or its contractors are the maintaining agency.

## Who to contact

- For **most city roads** (internal and CRMP): report to **GHMC** — [How to lodge a complaint](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint), helpline **21111111**. GHMC will route the complaint to the right zone or contractor.
- For **specific major corridors** under HMDA: you may need to check HMDA’s grievance system or contact GHMC, who can direct you.
