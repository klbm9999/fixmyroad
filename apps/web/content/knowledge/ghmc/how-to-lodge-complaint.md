You can report road-related issues (potholes, drainage, signage, lighting, encroachments, etc.) to the **Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC)** in the following ways.

## Helpline

- **GHMC Helpline: 21111111**  
  Call to register a complaint or enquiry. Have the location (road name, landmark, or area) and a brief description of the issue ready.

## Online complaint portal

- **GHMC grievance/complaint portal**: [https://igs.ghmc.gov.in](https://igs.ghmc.gov.in) (or the current URL as updated on the official GHMC website).  
  You may need to register or log in and then submit a complaint with:
  - Category (e.g. roads, drainage, street lights)
  - Location (address/area/landmark)
  - Description and, if possible, a photo

Check the portal for the exact steps (e.g. OTP verification, complaint number) and to track status.

## FixMyRoad

- You can also [report an issue](/report) on **FixMyRoad** with location and photo. Reports can be used to generate or support GHMC/RTO complaints and to help builders and the system prioritise repairs.

## What to include

- **Where**: Road name, stretch (e.g. between X and Y), landmark, or pin on map.
- **What**: e.g. pothole, blocked drain, missing sign, non-working light, encroachment.
- **When**: If it’s been like this for long or is monsoon-related, mention it.
- **Photo**: If possible, attach a clear photo so the issue can be verified and assigned quickly.

After lodging, note your complaint number (from the portal or helpline) so you can follow up. For official contact details and social media, see [GHMC contacts and socials](/knowledge/ghmc/ghmc-contacts-socials).
