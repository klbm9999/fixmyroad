The **Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC)** manages road maintenance for most of Hyderabad. A large share of major roads is maintained under the **Comprehensive Road Maintenance Programme (CRMP)** through zone-wise contracts with private agencies.

## What CRMP covers

Under CRMP contracts, the responsible agency typically undertakes:

- **Road re-carpeting**: Phased re-carpeting (e.g. 50% in year one, 30% in year two, 20% in year three) on the assigned road length.
- **Pothole filling and patch repair**: Timely repair of potholes and damaged patches to keep the road safe and rideable.
- **Drainage**: Cleaning of stormwater drains and sewerage manholes; keeping side drains and culverts clear.
- **Road cleanliness**: Sweeping and cleaning of roads, medians, and footpaths.
- **Monsoon emergency works**: Quick response to rain-related damage and waterlogging.
- **Road infrastructure**: Repairs and upkeep of related infrastructure as per the contract.
- **Grievance redressal**: Contractors are expected to have a mechanism to address complaints related to their stretch.

## Zone-wise packages

CRMP roads are divided into **zone-wise packages**. Each package is assigned to a contractor for a fixed period (e.g. five years). GHMC oversees the programme, approves works, and verifies quality (e.g. through Executive Engineers) before processing payments.

## Internal roads

A large length of **internal roads** (lanes and bylanes) in Hyderabad remains under **direct GHMC maintenance**, not under CRMP. These are also expected to be maintained for potholes, drainage, and basic upkeep.

For who maintains which roads and how to complain, see [Who maintains what](/knowledge/ghmc/who-maintains-what) and [How to lodge a complaint](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint).
