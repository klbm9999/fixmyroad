Contact details for the **Transport Department, Government of Telangana**, and **RTO offices** (e.g. Hyderabad) for licences, registration, and transport-related queries.

## Official website

**Transport Department, Telangana**: [https://transport.telangana.gov.in](https://transport.telangana.gov.in)

- Use the site for information on **vehicle registration**, **driving licences**, **fines**, **Acts and Rules**, and **district offices**.

## Hyderabad RTO (example)

- **RTO Code**: TS-14 (one of the Hyderabad RTOs)
- **Address**: Khairatabad Road, Opposite Eenadu Office, Raj Bhavan Quarters Colony, Somajiguda, Hyderabad 500004
- **Phone**: 040-233 11269

(Other RTO codes and addresses for Hyderabad and districts are listed on the Transport Department website under **District Offices**.)

## District offices

- **District offices list**: [https://transport.telangana.gov.in/html/aboutus-contactdirectory-district-offices.html](https://transport.telangana.gov.in/html/aboutus-contactdirectory-district-offices.html)  
  Use this to find the RTO/office for your area.

## Toll-free and support

- **Toll-free (app support)**: 1800 425 1110 or 1800 425 1100
- **General queries**: 040-233 70083
- **Support (10 am–6 pm)**: 040-233 70081
- **Alternate**: 040-233 70084

## Email

- **General**: support@tstransport.in
- **App support**: tapp-support@telangana.gov.in

## Acts and rules

- **Acts and Rules**: [https://transport.telangana.gov.in/html/actsrules.html](https://transport.telangana.gov.in/html/actsrules.html)  
  For the legal basis of traffic rules, licences, and penalties.

---

**Note**: For **road defects** (potholes, drainage, lights, markings), report to **GHMC** — see [How to lodge a complaint](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint). RTO/Transport handles rules, licences, and enforcement, not road maintenance.
