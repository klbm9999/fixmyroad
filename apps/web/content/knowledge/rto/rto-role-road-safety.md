The **Transport Department** of Telangana (and its RTO offices) is responsible for **vehicle registration**, **driving licences**, **traffic rules**, and **enforcement** under the Motor Vehicles Act and related rules. The **Rules of the Road Regulations, 1989** set out basic driving and traffic rules.

## What RTO / Transport does

- **Traffic rules**: Enforcing rules such as keep-left, overtaking, speed limits, and behaviour at junctions and pedestrian crossings.
- **Licences and registration**: Issue and renewal of driving licences and vehicle registration.
- **Penalties**: Fines and other penalties for traffic violations (e.g. overspeeding, wrong-side driving, drunk driving) as per the Motor Vehicles Act and state rules.
- **Road safety**: Awareness and coordination on road safety; sign standards and traffic management from a rules-and-enforcement perspective.

## What RTO does not do

- **RTO does not build or maintain the physical road** (carriageway, shoulders, drains, street lights). That is the responsibility of the **road-owning authority** — in Hyderabad, mostly **GHMC** (directly or through CRMP contractors), and for some corridors **HMDA** or other agencies.
- So for **potholes, drainage, road markings, signage installation, street lights**, the first contact is **GHMC** (or the relevant road authority), not RTO. RTO enforces traffic rules and may use signs that the road authority has installed.

## Summary

- **Road surface, drainage, lights, markings, signage (physical)**: Report to **GHMC** — see [How to lodge a complaint](/knowledge/ghmc/how-to-lodge-complaint).
- **Traffic rules, licences, enforcement**: **RTO / Transport Department** — see [RTO contacts](/knowledge/rto/rto-contacts).
