For pothole and patch repair as per IRC and good practice, the following **equipment and tools** are typically needed.

## Compaction

- **Roller**: Smooth-wheeled or vibratory roller for compacting the patch. For small patches, a **plate compactor** or **vibratory plate** may be used. Compaction is critical so the patch does not sink or ravel under traffic.
- **Tamping**: Hand tamper or mechanical tamper for edges and areas where the roller cannot reach.

## Hand tools

- **Cutting**: Pick, jackhammer, or saw to cut the patch boundary and remove failed material.
- **Cleaning**: Shovel, broom, and blower or water to clean the patch. The surface must be clean and dry before placing new mix.
- **Spreading**: Shovel and rake to spread the mix evenly and to the correct level.
- **Tack coat**: Brush or spray for applying tack coat on vertical sides (and base if required).

## Safety and traffic

- **Barricades and cones**: To mark the work zone and divert traffic.
- **Signs**: “Road work ahead”, “Slow”, or as per local traffic management requirements.
- **PPE**: Helmets, high-visibility vests, gloves, and safety shoes for workers. See [Safety steps](/knowledge/repair/safety).

## Minimum for small repairs

For **single potholes** or small patches (e.g. cold mix repair), minimum equipment often includes: cleaning tools, cold mix (or hot mix if available), hand tamper or plate compactor, and basic barricading and PPE. For **larger or structural repairs**, a roller and proper hot mix placement are needed as per IRC.

For full procedure, see [IRC pothole repair standards](/knowledge/irc/pothole-repair) and [Safety steps](/knowledge/repair/safety).
