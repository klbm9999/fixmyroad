Road repair work must be done with **traffic and worker safety** in mind. IRC and good practice recommend the following.

## Signage and barricading

- **Advance warning**: Place “Road work ahead” or similar signs so that traffic can slow down in time.
- **Work zone**: Delineate the work area with **cones**, **barricades**, or **barriers** so that traffic does not enter the work space.
- **Channelisation**: Guide traffic safely past the work zone (e.g. single lane, diversion) using signs and barriers. At night, use reflective devices or lights where possible.

## PPE (personal protective equipment)

- **Helmet**: For all workers on the road.
- **High-visibility vest**: So that workers are visible to drivers.
- **Gloves**: When handling tools and hot or sharp material.
- **Safety shoes**: To protect feet from injury and hot mix.
- **Eye protection**: Where there is risk of flying debris or splashes.

## Traffic management

- **Flag person**: Where needed, use a trained person to direct traffic and protect workers.
- **Speed**: Ensure traffic slows through the work zone; signs and barricades should be placed accordingly.
- **Parking**: Keep equipment and material off the live carriageway where possible; do not block sight lines or emergency access.

## First aid

- **First aid kit** should be available on site for minor cuts, burns, or injuries.
- **Serious injuries**: Have a plan to call for medical help (ambulance, nearest hospital). Workers should know the emergency number and location.

## Hot mix and plant safety

- Where **hot mix** is used, avoid skin contact and inhalation of fumes. Use appropriate gloves and work upwind where possible.
- **Plant and roller** operation should be by trained persons; keep others clear of moving equipment.
