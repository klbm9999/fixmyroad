For road repair and maintenance work in Hyderabad (including work under GHMC/CRMP), materials such as **bitumen**, **aggregates**, and **cold mix** are sourced from suppliers that meet specification and, where applicable, **GHMC approval**.

## Bitumen and aggregates

- **Bitumen**: Supplied by oil companies and authorised distributors. Quality and grade should match IRC/state specifications (e.g. VG-30, VG-40 for paving).
- **Aggregates**: Crushed stone and sand from approved quarries and crushers. Grading and quality should conform to the relevant IRC code for the layer (e.g. bituminous mix, base).
- **GHMC / CRMP**: For works under GHMC or CRMP contracts, contractors typically use **approved or empanelled vendors** and materials that pass quality checks. Lists of approved vendors may be updated by GHMC; check with the engineering wing or contract documents.

## Cold mix

- **Cold mix** (ready-to-use or bagged) for pothole patching is available from several manufacturers and dealers in **Telangana and Hyderabad**. Select products that meet IRC or state specifications.
- For **bulk or regular supply**, contact local construction material suppliers or manufacturers; for contract work, use vendors as per GHMC/CRMP approval.

## Contact points

- **GHMC**: For official lists of approved vendors or material standards for GHMC/CRMP works, contact the GHMC Engineering or Roads wing or refer to the contract/tender documents.
- **General suppliers**: Search for “bitumen suppliers Hyderabad”, “cold mix asphalt Hyderabad”, or “aggregate suppliers Telangana” and verify that materials meet the required specifications (IRC/state) before use.

For how to carry out repairs, see [IRC pothole repair standards](/knowledge/irc/pothole-repair), [Cold mix vs hot mix](/knowledge/repair/cold-vs-hot-mix), and [Required equipment](/knowledge/repair/equipment).
